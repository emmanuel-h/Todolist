# Privacy Policy for To Do List

**Effective Date:** August 2026

## 1. Overview

To Do List is a simple task-list application for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## 2. What Data We Collect

**We collect no personal data.**

Specifically, To Do List:
- Does not require an account or login
- Does not collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Does not track your location, device ID, or advertising ID
- Does not integrate with analytics or crash-reporting services
- Does not contain advertising
- Makes no network requests of any kind

## 3. How Data Is Stored

All app data — your list names, the items inside them, their completion state, and any target or due dates you set — is stored **locally on your device only**, in a private database that other apps cannot read. This data:

- Is never sent to us or to any server we control
- Is never shared with third parties
- Is never sold or used for marketing
- Remains entirely under your control

You can delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings.

### Android Auto Backup

To Do List uses Android's standard Auto Backup feature, which is provided by the operating system rather than by us. If you have device backup enabled in your Android settings, your device may copy the app's data to **your own personal Google Drive account**, where it is used to restore your lists when you reinstall the app or set up a new device.

We have no access to these backups. They belong to your Google account and are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can disable this at any time in your device's backup settings.

## 4. Permissions We Request

To Do List requests only the permissions it needs to deliver reminders:

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — to show you a reminder when a list is due today or scheduled for tomorrow. Reminders are generated entirely on your device from data you entered. Declining this permission disables reminders; every other feature keeps working.
- **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) — to re-schedule your daily reminder after the device restarts, since Android clears pending alarms on reboot.

Neither permission is used to collect or transmit information.

## 5. Third-Party Services

To Do List does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app contains no embedded trackers, SDKs, or external APIs that collect information about you or your usage.

## 6. Children's Privacy

To Do List is suitable for users of all ages. Since we collect no data whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or the digital age of consent in your jurisdiction).

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon publication. Your continued use of To Do List constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
